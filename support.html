Help and support

Having a problem when using Vincent OS? Don't worry, we're here to help! Our community is ready to assist you with any issues you may encounter. Just reach out to us and we'll do our best to resolve your problem!

How to contact us?

Forum

The v38armageddon Forum is the best place to ask for help. You can create a topic in the Vincent OS section and we will be happy to assist you.

Go to the forum

GitHub

You can open an issue on GitHub to report a bug. It's the best way to report a problem and get help from the community.

Open an issue

Reddit

If you prefer to use Reddit to explain your problem, you can join the subreddit r/VincentOS.

Join r/VincentOS

E-mail

If you prefer, you can send us an e-mail. For a security issue, please use the security address.

vincent-os@v38armageddon.net

security@v38armageddon.net
